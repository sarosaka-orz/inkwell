What it does

Everything you need.
Nothing in the way.

01

Live WYSIWYG

Markdown markers disappear as you move away and return the moment you edit.

02

Three modes

Write, read, or drop into the raw source with line numbers. One shortcut switches.

03

Math and diagrams

KaTeX and Mermaid render inline; GFM tables and task lists feel native.

04

Seven themes

Ink, Paper, Tokyo Night, Nord, Dracula, GitHub Dark, and GitHub Light.

05

Quiet by default

One slim bar, a drawer that stays out of the way, and a caret that glides.

06

Yours offline

Local files, bundled fonts, no account. Your writing never leaves the page.
